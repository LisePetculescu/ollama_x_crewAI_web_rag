Copenhagen Attractions Quiz

1. What is the name of the famous colorful harbor district in Copenhagen?
A) Christianshavn
B) Nyhavn
C) Vesterbro
D) Nørrebro

Answer: B) Nyhavn

--------------------------------------------------

2. Which Copenhagen attraction is one of the oldest amusement parks in the world?
A) Bakken
B) Tivoli Gardens
C) Copenhagen Zoo
D) Reffen

Answer: B) Tivoli Gardens

--------------------------------------------------

3. Which famous fairy tale author inspired the Little Mermaid statue?
A) Hans Christian Andersen
B) William Shakespeare
C) Søren Kierkegaard
D) Karen Blixen

Answer: A) Hans Christian Andersen

--------------------------------------------------

4. Which palace is the official residence of the Danish royal family?
A) Rosenborg Castle
B) Christiansborg Palace
C) Amalienborg Palace
D) Frederiksborg Castle

Answer: C) Amalienborg Palace

--------------------------------------------------

5. What can visitors see inside Rosenborg Castle?
A) Viking ships
B) The Danish crown jewels
C) Modern art exhibitions
D) A large aquarium

Answer: B) The Danish crown jewels

--------------------------------------------------

Bonus Question

6. Which Copenhagen attraction is famous for its spiral outdoor staircase and panoramic city views?
A) Church of Our Saviour
B) Round Tower
C) Copenhagen City Hall
D) Marble Church

Answer: A) Church of Our Saviour
